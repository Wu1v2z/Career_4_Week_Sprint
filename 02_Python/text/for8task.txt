Hello! My name is Leo, and this is my fish, Fin.

Fin is my pet. He is orange and white, and he lives in a small glass bowl. In the bowl, there is water, some green grass, and one blue rock.

Every morning, I give Fin food. The food is small brown flakes, and he eats them very quickly. In the evening, I turn on a small light near the bowl. I think he likes it.

Fin doesn’t play with toys. He can’t run or jump like other pets. He just swims around the bowl, again and again. Sometimes he swims near the rock, sometimes near the grass.

I like watching Fin. It’s relaxing, and he always makes me smile.
